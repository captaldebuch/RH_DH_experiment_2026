Four Research Routes (167 Years of Intuition)

The Riemann Hypothesis has inspired 26 distinct mathematical approaches across four conceptual routes. We present them chronologically, then show how our current work selects three routes for formalization.

The Four Routes

🟢 Analytic Number Theory (13 approaches)

Direct attack via zeta function, L-functions, bounds, and asymptotic formulas.

Current Status: 50% formalized (H13+H14). Philosophers: Riemann (1859), Piltz (1884), Riesz (1916), Pólya (1926), Speiser (1934), Turán (1948), de Bruijn (1950), Weil (1952), Newman (1976), Montgomery (1980), Robin (1984), Lagarias (2002), Farmer et al. (2007), Connes (2026).

🔷 Functional Analysis (2 approaches)

Closure and density problems in Hilbert spaces; Nyman-Beurling criterion. Our current focus.

Current Status: 67% formalized (Phase NB). Philosophers: Beurling & Nyman (1950), Báez-Duarte (2003).

🔵 Spectral / Quantum Physics (9 approaches)

Physical interpretation: zeros as eigenvalues of self-adjoint operators or quantum systems.

Current Status: Research open. Philosophers: Hilbert & Pólya (1910), Montgomery (1973), Katz & Sarnak (1999), Connes (1999), Berry & Keating (1999), Keating & Snaith (2000), de Branges (1986), Bender et al. (2017), Connes & Moscovici (2022).

🟡 Probabilistic (1 approach)

Random walk heuristics, GUE, statistical behavior of primes.

Current Status: Heuristic. Philosophers: Denjoy (1950).

Our Current Selection (2026)

Route 1: Functional Equivalence (Primary)

Nyman-Beurling criterion + Báez-Duarte discretization. Formalized as Phase NB, currently achieving 67% of RH reduction.

Key Achievement: Exact Fourier decomposition (NB12), rational jump cancellations verified, master reduction now requires three precisely-bounded frontier problems.

Route 2: Analytic Foundation (Support)

Robin divisor bounds + de la Vallée Poussin zero-free region. Classical analytic number theory.

Key Achievement: Structural framework in place; PNT-chain completion needed for SmoothMertensDecay.

Route 3: Spectral (Research Open)

Berry-Keating, Connes' prolate, PT-symmetric operators. Still active research.

Current Status: Not formalized; awaits theoretical breakthrough.

Frontier Characterization (September 2026)

What remains to complete the Functional Route:

  1. FourierRemainderLogEstimate: Uniform signed cross-modulus estimate along M = ⌊log(N)⁵⌋ + 1. Exact identity proved; decay bounds open.
  2. SmoothMertensDecay: Classical Mertens bound from PNT + zero-free region. Standard analytic number theory; Lean formalization incomplete.
  3. SignedBilinearDispersionDecay: Signed cancellation in Möbius bilinear kernel. RH-equivalent. No established technique.

All three are precisely formalized in Lean. None are false axioms or hidden sorries. The frontier is honest.
